Phone calls: Twilio

  1. In the Twilio console home page, copy your Account SID and Auth Token into the boxes here.
  2. On Overview, press Connect phone bridge. The public address fills in automatically. Keep Glitch running; you can minimise its window. For regular customer use, configure a stable HTTPS address.
  3. Press Check connection, choose your Twilio number, and press Send inbound calls to the app.
  4. Trial account: in the Twilio console, add your mobile under Phone Numbers → Verified Caller IDs, and allow India under Voice → Settings → Geo permissions. Trial calls start with a short Twilio message; press any key to hear the agent.
  5. In the Call workspace, set Phone line to Real calls through Twilio, add your mobile as a contact and press Add & call now. Keep the app open during calls.

Speed and quality test (A/B)

      
Runs the same 3-turn call through every brain on your Groq account: a price question in Hinglish, a request to switch to Marathi, and a Marathi answer. It also makes the same Marathi sentence with each Sarvam voice. It measures how fast the first words are ready, checks the answers, and recommends the fastest setup that still gets them right. Uses your real keys: about 12 Groq requests and 4 Sarvam clips.

      

      

      

    

    

      
App self-test

      
Runs every call flow with simulated customers and a simulated AI: outbound and inbound calls, the IVR, language switching, errors, real-call phone replies, assignment and WhatsApp. It uses no keys, makes no calls and sends no messages. Your data is backed up first and restored afterwards.
